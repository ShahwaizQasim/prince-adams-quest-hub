# Prince Adam's Realm

Create a premium, literary dark fantasy author and book website for 'Prince Adam’s Quest' by Terrence W. Walsh (Bent Nail Press).

Brand & Identity:
- Domain: princeadamsquest.com with Bent Nail Press branding and placeholder for registered trademark logo.
- Aesthetic: Deep forest green, rich burnished gold, dark charcoal, and warm parchment/cream tones. Elegant serif typography for headings with crisp modern sans-serif body text, subtle fantasy styling and gold badges.
- Award: Gold Medal Winner, Literary Titan Book Awards (2026).

Pages & Features Required:
1. Home:
   - Hero: Headline "Prince Adam’s Quest", opening hook quote: “Prince Adam always won.”, compelling book teaser, 2026 Literary Titan Gold Medal badge, CTA buttons ("Buy on Amazon", "Read Sample", "Discussion Guide").
   - Book overview card with cover art placeholder, pricing ($12.99 trade paperback / $7.99 ebook), 167 pages, ISBN: 979-8-9924602-3-0.
   - Core Themes section: Truth vs Propaganda, Satire of the Hero's Journey, Racism & Misogyny, Justice, and "What happens when a fool becomes king?".
   - Author spotlight teaser for Terrence W. Walsh with link to Substack.
   - Amazon Buy section with direct link to https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H4NM2THY.
   - Newsletter signup for Terrence's free Substack series "Lessons from the Keyboard".

2. About the Book:
   - Full polished synopsis based on the uploaded document: Originating from "Prince Adam always won" and disproving it, Truth as an absolute objective reality, Minnow the apprentice bard, King Biter claiming Adam's shiny boots, the troll Squire, the dragon Lysander, detour to Mineralia, and the question of history, comedy, or tragedy.
   - Character index: Prince Adam, Minnow (apprentice bard), King Biter & Fox, Squire the Troll, Lysander the Dragon.
   - Book specs & Literary Titan Gold Medal feature.
   - Sample excerpt reader preview modal or section.

3. About the Author:
   - Terrence W. Walsh biography: 30-year retired U.S. Coast Guard officer, poet, playwright, and fantasy author living on Cape Cod and keeping bees.
   - Highlights of other works: short comedy play 'ROUND TUIT' published by Brooklyn Publishers (Judge Lynch in County of Over-and-Undershot at www.brookpub.com).
   - "Lessons from the Keyboard" Substack showcase with link to subscribe for free.

4. Discussion Guide / Reader's Guide:
   - Complete 15 discussion questions from the uploaded guide in an interactive, clean accordion layout with search/filter and a print/download feature:
     1. Untested young man vs monster traditional patterns deviations.
     2. Short story concept expanded to novella with trolls and Minnow.
     3. Minnow's rhymes and song lyrics skewering Adam and emphasizing themes.
     4. King Biter's herald Fox testing Minnow on distinguishing true statements from false.
     5. 13 named unnumbered chapters.
     6. "Day Two" chapter: Minnow's monologue on migrants ("Abide") and xenophobia.
     7. "Fog Bank" chapter: Minnow growing into an independent heroine.
     8. The troll Squire taking Adam to the cleaners (justice, malice, comeuppance, grift).
     9. Squire enlisting dragon Lysander to hold unappealable court.
     10. Extensive back matter: guild of squires rules, dragon hierarchy, glossary, Minnow's lyrics, Xray references.
     11. Emerging friendship vs shared timeline between Adam and Minnow.
     12. Timeline hours from dying messenger in Leftovria to Lysander departing.
     13. Ending classification: history, comedy, or tragedy.
     14. Final chapter dance marathon with the four male bards of Leftovria.
     15. High fantasy vs low fantasy classification.
